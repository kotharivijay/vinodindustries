Notes

Marka = lot suffix: the part after the last - in the lot no (MMI-429-MAHI → MAHI, SST-675-LOVE → LOVE). Lots with no suffix fall into a — (no marka) group.

Toggle behaviour: the middle grouping level swaps between Quality and Marka; party totals and lots are unchanged. Party subtitle switches "N quality" ↔ "N marka". Search still matches party / lot / and now marka.

Toggle placement: first pill in the header action row (before PC Pali), teal when on — matches the Marka toggle already on the fold cards. State can persist in sessionStorage like the expand state does.
