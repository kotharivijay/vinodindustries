Delivery Challan — Extra-Charges toggle (per challan)

One switch decides whether the Extra Charges column + summary shows or hides — for both the on-screen challan view and the printed PDF. Transport / LR column stays either way (it's delivery info, not billing). A party-level default sets the switch's initial position when the challan is first opened.

3 · Where the default lives + how it persists
Party master field

A new checkbox on the party edit form: “Bill extra charges (freight / checking) on delivery challans.” Off means no chips, no summary — even if the operator forgets to toggle.

Party: M.Milan Industries
Bill extra charges on DC
Per-challan override

The switch at the top of the challan starts at the party default but can be flipped for a single challan (e.g. friendly delivery). The chosen state is saved on the challan row (showExtraCharges boolean).

Print & PDF follow

Print & Share PDF both read challan.showExtraCharges. Off → drop the Extra Charges column, remove the F×N / C×N chips from the grand-total row, and skip the "Extra Charges applicable" summary line. Same helper decides both.
