Which to build?

A · Popover

Compact. Doesn't hide the header. Awkward on phone screens (covers most of the viewport).

Best for: desktop-heavy use.
B · Pill row (recommended)

Zero clicks to see options. Swipeable on phone, single tap sets the filter, chip counts double as info at a glance. Batches × than baked into every pill.

Best for: ≤ ~20 parties (your current count).
C · Bottom sheet

Feels native on phone (thumb reach, familiar gesture). More clicks (button → sheet → pick) but scales past 30 parties.

Best for: mobile-heavy use or 30+ parties.
