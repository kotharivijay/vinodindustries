Recommendation

Variant A reuses the exact expand pattern already in the Fold-wise section — zero new UI language, works on mobile (bottom-sheet on small screens), and each slip links to its full page. Best default.

Variant B is the stronger analysis tool — same-shade slips side by side with a Δ/T column highlighting which chemical made one slip costlier. Best with 2–4 slips; needs horizontal scroll beyond that.

Suggested: ship A as the popup, with a small “Compare” toggle inside it that switches to B's matrix when 2+ slips are selected.
